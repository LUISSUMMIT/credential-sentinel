What it does

A date you can see getting closer is a date you don’t miss.

Most things don’t expire with a warning. They expire on a Tuesday, in a drawer — and you find out at the counter, at the border, or during an inspection.

  • A bar that fills as time runs outGreen through amber to dark red. Urgency is the greater of how far through its validity an item is and how few days are left — so a ten-year passport with six weeks to go looks exactly as alarming as it should.
  • Renewal windowsSome things can only be renewed in the last 90 days, or during the same season every year. Tell the app the rule and it tells you whether the window opens later, is open now, or has closed.
  • Groups you name yourselfPersonal, Business, Family, one per client. Each with its own color and symbol. Deleting a group never deletes the paperwork inside it.
  • Fifteen categoriesIDs, driver’s licenses, passports, vehicles, insurance, professional licenses, certifications, subscriptions, memberships, warranties, health coverage, financial, contracts, home and utilities, and other.
  • A widget on every surfaceHome Screen and Lock Screen on iPhone and iPad, Notification Center on Mac. Small, medium and large. Your next expirations without opening anything.
  • Reminders that arrive early enoughScheduled on your device from the dates you entered, with sensible defaults per category — and no push server anywhere in the loop.
  • Face ID before a deletionEditing and deleting are gated behind Face ID, Touch ID or your device password, so a misplaced tap can’t quietly erase a record you depend on.
  • Your devices, in stepEverything syncs through your own private iCloud account, so what you add on your iPhone is on your Mac. No account to create, nothing to log in to.

Better together

Works with Pinnacle POS.

Pinnacle POS is Summit’s point-of-sale app for iPhone and iPad. When Credential Sentinel and Pinnacle are on the same device, Credential Sentinel can share a read-only copy of the items you track — so the licenses, insurance policies and registrations that keep a business open sit right beside the business itself.

  • Read-only. Pinnacle can show your items; it can’t change or delete them. Credential Sentinel stays the only app that writes.
  • On your device. The copy lives in a container the two apps share on your own device. It is never sent to us or to any server.
  • Entirely optional. If you don’t use Pinnacle POS, nothing changes for you.
