Getting started

Adding your first item

Tap or click +, give the item a name, pick a category and set the expiration date. Everything else — the holder, a reference number, notes, a renewal website — is optional. The moment you save, the item appears in your list, the progress bar starts filling, and reminders are scheduled.

What the colors mean

Each bar runs from green through amber to dark red. Urgency is the greater of two things: how far an item is through its own validity period, and how close it is in absolute terms — anything inside roughly four months begins to color up regardless of how long it was valid for. That is deliberate. A ten-year passport with six weeks left should look exactly as alarming as a one-year permit with six weeks left.

Groups

Groups are buckets you name yourself — Personal, Business, Family, one per client — each with its own color and symbol. An item belongs to at most one group, the way a calendar event belongs to one calendar. Deleting a group never deletes the items inside it; they simply become ungrouped. On Mac you can drag an item straight onto a group in the sidebar, or onto “No Group” to unfile it.

Renewal windows

Some things can only be renewed during a particular stretch of time. Set a renewal window and the App will tell you whether it opens later, is open until a date, or has closed:

Common questions

The widget says “Nothing tracked yet” or asks me to open the App

The widget reads a small snapshot that the App writes for it. Open the App once and bring it to the foreground; the snapshot is rewritten at every launch and whenever you save. The widget then catches up at its next refresh. If it still looks empty, remove the widget and add it again.

My items are not appearing on my other device

Check, on both devices, that you are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on for Credential Sentinel. Then open Settings inside the App: it reports the iCloud account status and whether a synced store was opened. Sync is not instantaneous — give it a minute with both devices online and unlocked.

Settings says changes are not being saved

That warning means the App could not open its normal storage and has fallen back to temporary memory, so anything you add will be lost when you quit. Restart the App; if the warning persists, restart the device and then email us the exact text shown under Storage in Settings.

I saved an item and it vanished

It almost certainly saved. The default view on Mac is Needs Attention, which only shows items expiring within about 90 days, so something further out will not appear there. A banner should tell you so and offer to open it — otherwise switch to All Items.

I am not getting reminders

Allow notifications for Credential Sentinel in your device settings, and check that Focus or Scheduled Summary is not holding them back. iOS also caps how many future reminders any app may queue, so the App keeps the nearest ones; very distant items get their reminders scheduled as they approach.

Why does it ask for Face ID when I edit something?

To stop a tap in the wrong place from quietly rewriting or deleting a record you rely on. Archiving is one swipe; deleting needs both a confirmation and authentication. Both gates can be turned off in Settings.

What is the Pinnacle section in Settings?

Pinnacle POS is Summit’s point-of-sale app for iPhone and iPad. When both apps are on the same device, Credential Sentinel keeps a read-only copy of the items you track in a container the two apps share, so Pinnacle POS can display them. Settings → Pinnacle shows whether that shared container is reachable and has a Publish to Pinnacle now button. Pinnacle POS cannot change or delete anything in Credential Sentinel, and nothing leaves your device. If you don’t use Pinnacle POS, you can ignore this section. More about Pinnacle POS.

Can I share a group with someone else?

Not yet. Sync today covers your own devices through your own iCloud account. Sharing a whole group with another person is the next significant feature, and groups were built first precisely because a group is the thing you would share.

Can I attach a scan of the document?

Not yet. You can add a web address to the item today — the renewal portal, for instance. Attachments are on the roadmap.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the App from each device, and remove its iCloud data under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

Requirements

iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later on iPhone and iPad; macOS 26 or later on Mac. iCloud sync requires being signed in to an Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled.

Report a bug or request a feature
