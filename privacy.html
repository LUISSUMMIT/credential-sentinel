Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version. Credential Sentinel has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you have iCloud turned on, in your own private iCloud account. The developer cannot see your data, and no third party receives it.

This policy explains how Credential Sentinel (“the App”) handles information. The App is published by Summit (“we”, “us”), based in Florida, United States. It applies to the iPhone, iPad and Mac versions of the App and to its widget.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, transmit, store, sell or share any personal information. We operate no server, no database and no account system for the App. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

We have no technical means of accessing what you track in the App.

2. Information you enter, and where it lives

The App exists to track expiration dates. You may choose to enter information such as:

  • A title or description of the item (for example “Passport”, “Shop insurance”)
  • The holder’s name, if you track items for more than one person
  • A reference or document number
  • Issue, expiration and renewal dates, and renewal windows
  • A category and an optional group name you choose
  • Free-form notes
  • An optional web address for the renewal site

This content is stored:

  • On your device, in the App’s private storage area, protected by the operating system and by your device passcode and encryption.
  • In your own private iCloud database, if you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled for the App. Synchronization uses Apple CloudKit and goes only to the private database of your Apple Account, so your devices can see the same items. We have no access to that database. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • In a shared container on your device, so the widget can display your next expirations without opening the App. This file never leaves your device.

If you also install one of our companion apps on the same device, the App may publish a read-only copy of this same information into that shared on-device container so the companion app can display it. This stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us.

You decide what to enter. If you would rather not store a sensitive document number in the App, you can track the item by title and date alone — the App works perfectly well that way.

3. What the App does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party software development kits, no social logins, no marketing tools.
  • No network requests to us or to anyone else. The App contains no code that uploads your content anywhere other than Apple’s iCloud on your behalf.
  • No reading of your contacts, photos, location, calendars, health data, microphone or camera.

4. Face ID, Touch ID and your passcode

You may enable a biometric gate so that editing or deleting an item requires Face ID, Touch ID or your device password. This uses Apple’s Local Authentication framework. Your fingerprint and face data never leave the secure hardware of your device and are never made available to the App or to us — the App is told only whether the check succeeded or failed.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device from the dates you entered. No push server is involved, and no notification content passes through us or any third party.

6. Links you add

If you add a renewal web address to an item and tap it, your device opens that address in your browser. The site you visit is then subject to its own privacy practices. We receive nothing about it.

7. Apple’s role

Downloads, purchases and app-level diagnostics are handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with developers in your device settings, Apple may show us anonymized, aggregated crash reports and download counts. Those reports contain no content you entered and do not identify you.

8. Children

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

9. Your control over your data

You want toHow
Delete one itemDelete it in the App (confirmation and, if enabled, biometric authentication are required).
Delete everything on a deviceDelete the App from that device.
Delete the iCloud copyOn iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. On Mac: System Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage. Remove the App’s data there.
Stop syncingTurn off iCloud Drive for the App in your device settings. Items already on the device remain.
Export or move your dataYour data remains on your own devices and in your own iCloud account at all times; there is no export request to make of us.

Because we hold no data about you, requests to access, correct, port or erase personal information under the GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or similar laws cannot be fulfilled by us — there is nothing in our possession to act on. Your rights are exercised directly on your device and in your Apple Account.

10. Security

Your data is protected by your device’s own encryption and passcode, by the App’s optional biometric gate, and — for the iCloud copy — by Apple’s encryption of iCloud data in transit and at rest. Keep your device passcode private and your operating system up to date. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but your information is never exposed to us or to any server we control, because none exists.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the App’s release notes. Continuing to use the App after a change takes effect means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in the App: support@summituspro.com. We aim to reply within two business days.
